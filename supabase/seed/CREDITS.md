# Seed images

Used only by the local seed, the screenshots and the public site.

- `profile-photos/*/0.jpg`: faces generated by AI (StyleGAN, via
  thispersondoesnotexist.com). They are not real people.
- `profile-photos/*/1.jpg` and `event-covers/`: party photos from
  [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, no attribution
  required), cropped and resized.
